# Closed-test scope and known issues

**Original macOS accessibilitySelectedChildren SIGSEGV: OPEN / UNRESOLVED.**
The historical failure was observed on the retained Apple Silicon Mac/Qt
installation. Later successful launches do not prove it fixed. Candidate02's
separate Create Wallet settings abort and newer callback repairs have different
demonstrated causes. Normal testing does not disable accessibility or require
debugger instrumentation. If recurrence occurs, retain this candidate's exact
hash, action sequence and matching macOS crash report; share only after privacy
review through the assigned private channel.

Saved-request A-to-B and generated watch-only wallet switching require exact
observed/manual screen evidence. Prior unresponsive automation is not itself
proof of a selector bug. A reproduced wrong-wallet authority defect blocks the
affected candidate capability; it is not an acceptable cosmetic limitation.

Transient HTTPS reads may refuse while the B3 tip is reconciling. One newly
captured vault_operations HTTP400 had that exact reason. Older responses whose
bodies were not retained cannot all be retrospectively assigned this cause.
Preserve last certified data and uncertainty rather than inventing current
state. Never bypass anchor/reconciliation safety to make a read succeed.

Qualification limits: no WAN/controlled-load, QUIC or operator encryption,
machine power-loss, unflushed historical BDB log replay, universal historical
or encrypted legacy migration, Windows directory durability, Intel/older Mac
or external notarized-distribution qualification is implied. App dSYM matches
the package; upstream Qt source-level dSYMs may not be available. Exact bundled
Qt images/hashes remain in the dependency manifest for crash correlation.

Generated-fixture flat-file rename-gap recovery is explicit offline recovery,
not automatic recovery. DirectoryCommit/power-loss limits remain. Never rewrite
nTime/txids/scripts/outpoints, reset journals or use a current-UTXO total as a
claim of complete history. Keep originals and verified backups.

Owner-identified wrong-fork community installations are attributed only to
those identified cases. Unmeasured unrelated reports remain separate. These
wallet patches do not prove why another reported height or percentage stalled.
Read-only diagnostics must establish build, blocks/headers/hash, peer-download
state, finality and actual tip advancement; peer count is not synchronization.

Future features excluded: stable-asset trading fees, new market identities,
automatic validator removal, quorum/consensus changes, hosting commissions,
bridge changes, QUIC and new BFT/PoW. Existing canonical colored-asset/B3
economics, B3 fee currency, eligibility and durable signing remain unchanged.

No public production release, external binary handoff or always-on service is
approved by this local candidate. Actual test results and retained earlier
passes must remain labelled separately in the release-readiness report.
